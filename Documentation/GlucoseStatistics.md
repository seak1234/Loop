# Glucose statistics

The Glucose page shows a compact visual summary above its existing chart and prediction inputs. The buttons across the top select 3, 7, 14, 30 or 90 days directly; they never open settings. The selected button stays highlighted. Pull down to refresh.

The glucose diagram is a stacked bar showing time below, in and above the saved range. The adjacent legend gives percentages and boundaries, and a large percentage highlights time in range. Average glucose and a short coverage/source caption provide context. Missing data leaves a neutral bar. The insulin diagram shows the recorded basal/bolus split when basal history is sufficiently complete, with recorded daily average and totals below. Incomplete history is labeled; the diagram stays neutral when a split cannot be established. The metric grid and long explanatory footers are removed.

Below Average glucose, the card shows **GMI** and **Est. HbA1c** as percentages with one decimal place. GMI uses `3.31 + 0.02392 × mean glucose (mg/dL)` ([GMI study](https://doi.org/10.2337/dc18-1581)); the separate ADAG-based HbA1c estimate uses `(mean glucose (mg/dL) + 46.7) / 28.7` ([ADAG study](https://doi.org/10.2337/dc08-0545)). Both use the selected period's time-weighted CGM mean and require at least 14 days and 70% observed coverage. Below those thresholds the rows show a dash and explain the data requirement. They are labeled as CGM estimates; laboratory HbA1c can differ. They never enter dosing or alert calculations.

An **AGP · Typical day** card sits between the statistics and Predicted Glucose cards and follows the same selected period, glucose units and range-shading setting. It shows a local-time 24-hour median, a darker 25th–75th percentile band and a lighter 5th–95th percentile band, following the distribution display described by the [International Diabetes Center](https://www.agpreport.org/agp/agpreports). This implementation first takes each local day's median in each 15-minute clock-time slot, then computes linearly interpolated percentiles across those daily medians, so one densely sampled day cannot outweigh others. It requires three distinct observed local dates per slot; unsupported slots remain gaps and are never connected or filled. Duplicate timestamps, invalid/out-of-window readings and daylight-saving repeated hours cannot inflate the number of days. The caption identifies the period and CGM coverage and recommends 14+ days; shorter profiles are exploratory. The graph uses actual CGM history only, not forecast values, and has explicit loading/insufficient-data states and a VoiceOver distribution summary.

The cell builds its controls and diagrams once. History updates change existing labels and bar fills without reloading the table section. Captions reserve two lines, and estimated/final row heights share a measurement cached per width and text size. Statistics, AGP aggregation and sample merging run outside the main UI thread; completed loads also precompute the other day selections.

The “Settings” button at the top right beside the “Glucose” page title opens the glucose boundaries, Nightscout-history toggle, and optional graph shading. The prediction chart is labeled “Predicted Glucose”; the statistics card no longer includes a separate settings link. Existing saved boundaries and shading preferences carry over. Boundaries also drive journal statistics and shading on both the dashboard and prediction chart. These settings never enter dosing or alert calculations.

Each CGM reading represents time until the next reading, capped at five minutes and clipped to the selected reporting window. Gaps are excluded from the percentages and reduce coverage. Manual/display-only readings are excluded. Local samples take precedence over Nightscout samples within two minutes; exact timestamps are deduplicated. History is cached for the app session, separately for requested periods. Cached values appear immediately, including when older than five minutes; stale values refresh in the background. Refreshing a shorter period preserves already downloaded longer periods. Local glucose, local insulin, Nightscout glucose, and Nightscout treatments load independently and publish as each becomes available. Nightscout glucose uses three-day windows with at most four glucose requests in flight, publishing each completed window. In-flight requests cannot overwrite a subsequently selected period. The cache resets when the device manager, Nightscout connection, or inclusion setting changes.

Finalized normalized local insulin doses are used, with basal clipped to the window; pending delivery is excluded. Basal/suspension intervals establish insulin coverage. Nightscout logged boluses supplement only calendar days with no local insulin delivery records, avoiding duplicate mirrored treatment totals. They never supply basal. The insulin split is withheld with less than 90% basal history or any remote-only bolus days. Missing records show a dash.

Credentials use the existing keychain accounts and never enter the statistics output. The existing read-only Nightscout reader handles authentication, split queries, record validation, and same-origin redirects. Loading/failure/connection messages and coverage remain visible when data is incomplete.

Validation: `bash Scripts/test-glucose-statistics.sh` runs the same calculation tests included in LoopTests without requiring Xcode. `bash Scripts/test-glucose-statistics-loader.sh` exercises cached range selection, expired-cache background refresh, progressive local/remote loading, bounded Nightscout requests, cache invalidation, pull-to-refresh, and stale-response handling using the production async loader with controlled stores. `bash Scripts/test-glucose-statistics-layout.sh` runs the production card and palette on the real Mac Catalyst UIKit runtime, checks stable geometry and persistent views at 320/390/430-point widths, verifies bar fills, estimate rows, day-button action wiring and AGP hierarchy/height stability, and renders loading, empty, populated and mmol/L profile states. Set `GLUCOSE_LAYOUT_OUTPUT_DIR` to preserve those PNGs. This checks the card, not the full iPhone app. `bash Scripts/typecheck-glucose-statistics.sh` checks the changed UIKit/SwiftUI screen and loader with Mac Catalyst SDK types and service/chart interfaces stubbed. A full iOS build and rendered device review require Xcode.
